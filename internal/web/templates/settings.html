Data protection

Your chats and documents are stored encrypted. Only your password can open them — a stolen copy of the gateway database (or a backup) reveals nothing.

Changing your password is safe (your data key is re-wrapped in the same step). But if an admin resets your password while you're signed out, the gateway can't re-wrap your data key and your encrypted chats and documents can't be recovered.
